# Lead Tracking Template — Alamir Pack

## Purpose
Use this template to record verified business inquiries generated by the Free Marketing experiments and connect content activity to real sales opportunities.

## Rules
- Record only inquiries that actually arrived and were verified.
- Do not invent attribution. If the source post is unknown, write `Unknown`.
- Do not put names, phone numbers, email addresses, private messages, or other identifying customer details in this repository.
- Keep customer contact details in the company's approved private system, if needed.
- Do not count likes or reach as leads. A lead requires a real inquiry from a potential business customer.

## Inquiry log
Add one row per distinct inquiry. Use `Not provided` when a detail is unknown; do not guess.

## Suggested stage values
- New inquiry
- Specifications being clarified
- Quotation requested
- Quotation sent
- Follow-up needed
- Order confirmed
- Closed / not qualified

## Review checklist
At each experiment review, summarize:
- Number of verified inquiries
- Number of qualified industrial leads
- Number of quotation requests
- Number of confirmed orders, if any
- Which post/source can be attributed with evidence
- Missing information and next action
