# Industry Discovery

## Purpose

This file defines the framework for discovering new industrial sectors in Egypt that may represent potential opportunities for Alamir Pack.

The goal is not to assume that a sector needs our products.

The goal is to discover, research, verify, and test real industrial needs.

---

## 1. Main Objective

Discover new industrial sectors in Egypt where:

* Paper cores may be used
* Carton corner protectors may be used
* Existing applications may be expanded
* New applications may exist
* Real customer demand may be present

The objective is to expand beyond the industries and applications that are already known to Alamir Pack.

---

## 2. Central Research Question

The main question is:

> Where else can Alamir Pack's products solve a real industrial need in Egypt?

Supporting questions include:

* Which industries use products similar to ours?
* Which industries have winding, rolling, packaging, storage, or transportation processes?
* Which industries may require cylindrical cores?
* Which industries may require edge protection?
* Which industries have recurring industrial consumption?
* Which industries have identifiable potential customers?
* Which industries show evidence of real demand?

---

## 3. Research Scope

The initial discovery phase focuses on Egypt.

Research may include:

* Manufacturing industries
* Processing industries
* Packaging industries
* Converting industries
* Textile industries
* Paper and cardboard industries
* Plastic-related industries
* Food-related industrial applications
* Construction-related industrial applications
* Logistics and shipping
* Agricultural and industrial packaging
* Other industrial sectors discovered through research

This list is not a limitation.

New sectors should be added whenever research reveals a potentially relevant application.

---

## 4. What We Investigate in Every Sector

For each potential sector, research should investigate:

### Industry

* What does the industry produce?
* How does the production process work?
* What materials are used?
* What products are manufactured?

### Product Usage

* Is a paper core used?
* Is edge protection used?
* What is the exact application?
* At which stage of production or logistics is it used?

### Customer

* Who actually purchases the product?
* Who makes the purchasing decision?
* What types of companies use it?
* Where are potential customers located?

### Demand

* Is there evidence of recurring demand?
* Is the industry active in Egypt?
* Are there enough potential companies to justify investigation?
* Is there evidence of current purchasing activity?

### Requirements

* What dimensions are required?
* What strength is required?
* What moisture requirements exist?
* Are there special technical requirements?
* Are there quality or certification requirements?

---

## 5. Discovering New Applications

A sector may be relevant even when its use of our products is not immediately obvious.

Research should therefore look for processes involving:

* Winding
* Rolling
* Rewinding
* Slitting
* Packaging
* Wrapping
* Storage
* Transportation
* Load stabilization
* Edge protection
* Industrial handling

The objective is to identify the actual industrial problem first, then determine whether one of Alamir Pack's products can solve it.

---

## 6. Discovering Potential Companies

Once a potentially relevant sector is identified, research should look for real companies operating in Egypt.

For each company, where information is available, record:

* Company name
* Industry
* Location
* Products manufactured
* Relevant application
* Potential product need
* Website or public source
* Evidence supporting the opportunity

The purpose is to move from:

**Industry → Companies → Real Potential Customers**

---

## 7. Evidence of Real Demand

A sector should not be considered a validated opportunity based only on technical compatibility.

Evidence may include:

* Existing use of paper cores
* Existing use of edge protection
* Active manufacturers
* Recurring industrial production
* Import activity
* Local purchasing activity
* Supplier activity
* Industry growth
* Public product specifications
* Customer requirements
* Multiple independent sources confirming the same application

The stronger the evidence, the stronger the opportunity hypothesis.

---

## 8. Technical Fit

For every promising opportunity, compare the identified requirements with Alamir Pack's official capabilities.

The comparison should consider:

* Product type
* Inner diameter
* Wall thickness
* Length
* Moisture requirements
* Strength requirements
* Dimensional accuracy
* Production requirements
* Delivery requirements

Alamir Pack's official company information must always come from the Start App / GitHub source of truth.

Do not invent technical capabilities that are not officially documented.

---

## 9. Competition

For each promising sector, research:

* Existing suppliers
* Local manufacturers
* Importers
* Alternative products
* Competitor positioning
* Customer purchasing patterns
* Potential barriers to entry

The objective is to understand the market, not simply to list competitors.

---

## 10. Customer Accessibility

A technically suitable sector may still be difficult to reach.

Research should therefore consider:

* Number of identifiable companies
* Geographic concentration
* Availability of company contact information
* Industry associations
* Professional networks
* Search visibility
* Possible sales channels
* Ability to identify decision-makers

The goal is to determine whether potential customers can realistically be reached.

---

## 11. Opportunity Validation

A sector becomes a serious opportunity hypothesis only when there is evidence for several of the following:

* Real industrial application
* Identifiable potential customers
* Evidence of demand
* Relevant product requirements
* Technical compatibility
* Commercial feasibility
* Accessible customers
* Limited or manageable barriers

The process is:

**Potential Sector → Research → Evidence → Opportunity Hypothesis → Validation → Test**

---

## 12. Opportunity Classification

Each discovered opportunity should be classified as:

### Confirmed Opportunity

Strong evidence exists and the opportunity is ready for practical testing.

### Promising Opportunity

Good evidence exists, but additional validation is needed.

### Research Needed

The sector appears relevant, but evidence is currently insufficient.

### Not Validated

Available evidence does not currently support the opportunity.

These classifications are research statuses, not permanent judgments.

---

## 13. What Happens After Discovery

When an opportunity becomes sufficiently supported, the next step is not automatically to spend money.

The next step should be a controlled test.

Possible tests may include:

* Identifying a small group of potential customers
* Researching their requirements
* Preparing targeted content
* Direct outreach
* Requesting feedback
* Testing a specific product/application message
* Measuring responses

Results should then return to the marketing system for analysis.

---

## 14. Discovery Record

Each meaningful discovery should eventually be documented using a consistent structure:

### Sector

Name of the industrial sector.

### Application

How the product may be used.

### Product

Paper core or carton corner protector.

### Potential Customers

Types or examples of companies that may need the product.

### Evidence

Sources and facts supporting the opportunity.

### Technical Requirements

Known or suspected product requirements.

### Competition

Known suppliers or alternatives.

### Uncertainties

What is still unknown?

### Opportunity Status

Confirmed Opportunity / Promising Opportunity / Research Needed / Not Validated.

### Next Test

The next practical action needed to validate the opportunity.

---

## 15. Research Rules

* Do not invent sectors, customers, demand, or technical requirements.
* Do not assume that technical compatibility means commercial demand.
* Do not assume that a large industry means a good opportunity.
* Do not rely on one weak source when stronger evidence is available.
* Clearly distinguish facts, findings, hypotheses, and unknowns.
* Record sources for important findings.
* Prefer current information when researching active industries.
* Use multiple sources when possible.
* Keep unsuccessful opportunities documented when they provide useful learning.
* Never change official Alamir Pack information based on external research.

---

## 16. Discovery Philosophy

The purpose of industry discovery is not to find as many industries as possible.

The purpose is to discover **real industrial problems where Alamir Pack may have a commercially useful solution.**

The process should remain open-minded:

**Do not start with the answer.**

Start with the industrial problem.

Then investigate the process.

Then identify the product requirement.

Then identify the customer.

Then verify demand.

Then test the opportunity.
