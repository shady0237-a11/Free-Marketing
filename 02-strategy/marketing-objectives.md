# Marketing Objectives

## 1. Main Marketing Goal

Build a practical, measurable digital marketing system for Alamir Pack that helps the company:

* Acquire new customers.
* Increase awareness of Alamir Pack and its products.
* Discover new markets, industries, applications, and business opportunities.
* Generate qualified leads and convert marketing activity into measurable commercial opportunities.
* Build a repeatable marketing process that improves through data and experience.

---

## 2. Market Expansion

Market expansion is a core objective of the marketing project.

The goal is not limited to reaching more customers within industries that already use Alamir Pack products. The marketing system should also actively search for:

* New industrial sectors that can use paper cores.
* New industrial sectors that can use carton corner protectors.
* New applications for existing products.
* New customer types with requirements that can potentially be served by Alamir Pack.
* New opportunities created by specific product characteristics, specifications, or manufacturing capabilities.

This expansion approach applies to both the Egyptian market and international markets.

A sector should not be treated as a confirmed opportunity simply because a product appears technically relevant. It should be researched and tested to determine whether there is real demand, identifiable customers, suitable requirements, and a realistic commercial opportunity.

---

## 3. Local Market — Egypt

A major objective is to expand Alamir Pack's reach within Egypt.

The marketing system should:

* Identify industrial sectors that currently use paper cores or carton corner protectors.
* Discover additional sectors that may have relevant applications for these products.
* Identify companies within those sectors that could represent potential customers.
* Understand the operational requirements and specifications of different sectors.
* Discover potential applications that are not currently part of the company's main target sectors.
* Test market interest before investing significant marketing resources in a new sector.

The local-market objective is therefore not simply to find "more customers."

It is to continuously discover **where else the company's products can create practical value** and determine which opportunities are commercially worth pursuing.

---

## 4. Export & International Expansion

International expansion is another major objective of the marketing system.

The project should research:

* Countries with potential demand for paper cores and carton corner protectors.
* Industrial sectors within those countries that use or may use these products.
* New applications and customer types that could create export opportunities.
* Import and market-demand signals where reliable data is available.
* Potential customer segments and companies within promising markets.
* Market-specific product requirements and commercial considerations.

International research should not be limited to countries or sectors already known to the company.

The objective is to systematically discover potential export opportunities and then evaluate them using evidence rather than assumptions.

---

## 5. Brand & Digital Presence

Strengthen Alamir Pack's digital presence so that potential customers can:

* Understand what the company manufactures.
* Understand the products and their applications.
* Recognize the company's industrial capabilities.
* Find relevant information easily.
* Contact the company through clear communication channels.
* Develop confidence in the company as a potential industrial supplier.

The digital presence should remain professional, consistent, practical, and aligned with the company's official information.

---

## 6. Lead Generation

Marketing activity should ultimately support the generation of qualified business opportunities.

The system should create a clear path from:

**Awareness → Interest → Inquiry → Qualification → Quotation → Customer**

Marketing should aim to generate leads that can be understood and tracked, rather than measuring success only through views, followers, or engagement.

Where possible, each lead should provide enough information to understand:

* Who the potential customer is.
* Which industry they operate in.
* What product they are interested in.
* What application they have.
* How they discovered Alamir Pack.
* What stage the opportunity has reached.

---

## 7. Measurement & Learning

Marketing decisions should increasingly be based on measurable results.

The system should track relevant indicators such as:

* Reach and awareness.
* Website visits.
* Inquiries.
* Quote requests.
* Qualified leads.
* New customers.
* Lead sources.
* Marketing channels.
* Content performance.
* Industry and sector response.
* Market and export research findings.

The purpose of measurement is not only to report results.

It is to create a continuous learning cycle:

**Analyze → Decide → Execute → Measure → Learn → Improve**

The knowledge gained from marketing experiments should be documented so that successful approaches can be repeated and unsuccessful approaches can be improved or discontinued.

---

## 8. Target Setting Rule

The project should not create arbitrary numerical marketing targets before sufficient data is available.

Initial objectives should focus on:

* Building the system.
* Establishing measurement.
* Collecting reliable baseline data.
* Testing channels and content.
* Discovering potential sectors and markets.
* Generating initial leads.
* Learning from real results.

Once enough reliable data has been collected, measurable targets can be established based on actual business and marketing performance.

---

## 9. Strategic Principle

The marketing system should continuously look beyond the company's current customer base and known applications.

The central question is:

> **Where else can Alamir Pack's products solve a real industrial need?**

This question applies across:

* Existing markets.
* New local industries.
* New international industries.
* New applications.
* New customer types.
* New geographic markets.

Any potential opportunity must be investigated using reliable evidence before being treated as a validated market opportunity.

---

## 10. Relationship to the Company Source of Truth

All company-specific facts used in marketing must remain consistent with the official Alamir Pack company information and the Start App repository, which is the company's official source of truth.

External research may be used to discover and evaluate information outside the company, including:

* Market size.
* Industry trends.
* Competitors.
* Target countries.
* Import and export data.
* Potential industries.
* Customer discovery.
* Search demand.
* Marketing channels.
* Industry benchmarks.

External research must not be used to invent or alter official company facts.
